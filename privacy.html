Home Ops Centre – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Home Ops Centre is a private household planning portal used only by Royce and Heidi Devenish and any family members they personally add. It is not offered to the public.

What the portal stores

Goals, habits, tasks, reading progress, meeting notes, checklists, receipts and similar planning information that the household members enter themselves. This is stored in the household's own Supabase database and is visible only to signed-in household members.

Google Calendar access

If the household chooses to connect Google Calendar, the portal asks for permission to view and edit events (the calendar.events permission). It uses this only to show calendar events inside the portal and to create, move or delete events that a household member asks it to. A sign-in token is stored securely on the server so the connection keeps working; it can be removed at any time by choosing Disconnect in the portal or by removing access in the Google Account settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share or disclose Google user data to anyone.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising.
  • We do not allow people outside the household to read it.

The portal's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

The portal can optionally read budget information from YNAB, and it is hosted on GitHub Pages with data held in Supabase. These services process data on the household's behalf under their own terms.

Deleting your data

Household members can delete their entries inside the portal, download a copy of their data from Settings, or ask the household administrator to remove their account and associated data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the household administrator, Royce Devenish.
